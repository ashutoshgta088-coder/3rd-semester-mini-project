✨ Core System Capabilities
📍
50-Meter GPS Geofencing
Calculates exact distance via Haversine algorithm from classroom coordinates. Students outside the 50m radius are blocked from marking.
👆
Biometrics & Passkeys
Integrated with WebAuthn API for Windows Hello, Touch ID, Face ID, and Android fingerprint sensors right from the browser.
📸
Approved Photo Identity
Every student profile photo undergoes faculty verification. Photo matching prevents proxy attempts during lecture sessions.
⚡
Dynamic Faculty QR Sessions
Teachers generate live sessions with time limits, customizable geofence radiuses, and high-contrast dynamic QR codes.
⚠️
Defaulter Tracking (<75%)
Instant detection of students falling below AKTU's mandatory 75% attendance threshold with automated warnings.
📥
Reports & Excel Export
Comprehensive attendance summaries with filters by subject, section, and date, plus instant download of formatted Excel spreadsheets.
